Game description

The identity of the Dark Lady from Shakespeare's sonnets has eluded scholars and historians for more than four centuries.

In Black Sonata, you will pursue the elusive Dark Lady through the streets and alleyways of Elizabethan London, catching glimpses and whispers that may yield clues to her identity. However, not all of the evidence is clear.

You will need to sharpen your wits if you are to finally unmask the Dark Lady before she slips quietly from the pages of history and is lost.

Based on the acclaimed solitaire board game from Side Room Games, Black Sonata delivers a rich deduction experience inspired by Shakespeare’s sonnets.

Each game presents a unique challenge, requiring careful observation, logical deduction, and strategic planning.

Features

  • Engaging solitaire gameplay
  • Hidden-movement mechanics
  • Challenging deduction puzzle
  • High replayability
  • Historically inspired setting and theme
